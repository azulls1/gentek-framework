# Story: CheckpointManager.run() returns notes alongside decision

**ID:** STORY-005
**Feature:** `specs/memory-system.md` (v0.4.5)
**Sprint:** S1-bugfixes
**Plan:** `plans/memory-bugfixes.md`

## Story
As **the orchestrator and (later) the scribe agent**, I want **`CheckpointManager.run()` to expose the human's approval notes in its return value**, so that **downstream consumers (state.checkpoints persistence in v0.4.5, scribe in v0.5.0) can use them without re-parsing internal state**.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] `CheckpointManager.run()` returns `{ decision: CheckpointDecision; notes?: string }` (changed from `CheckpointDecision`).
- [ ] `notes` comes from the handler's `notes` field when `decision === 'approve'`; otherwise `undefined`.
- [ ] `notes` is also `undefined` when modes `skip` or `auto` short-circuit the handler.
- [ ] All internal call-sites (currently only `Orchestrator.run()`) are updated to destructure the new shape.
- [ ] CHANGELOG documents the breaking change for external `@iagentek/core` consumers (none known today).
